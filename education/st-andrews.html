About

I studied Mathematics at the University of St Andrews for three years, and it was during this time that I realised my passion for data and computing. I always gravitated towards the modules that involved a heavy amount of statistics or programming.

Heading into the last semester of university, I was still unsure what career I wanted to pursue, but thankfully I had enrolled in Computing in Statistics where I made the definitive decision that my career would be in the intersection of these areas.

Activities & Societies

During my time at St Andrews I was a performance scholar, which meant I had to learn to balance my time between a tough academic course and a rigorous training and competition schedule.

My proudest achievement was in 2023 when my team won the UK Universities Championship. Golf is primarily an individual sport, but I enjoy the process that comes with winning as a team.
